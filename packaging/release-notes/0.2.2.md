Plico 0.2.2 lets you move, resize and recolour annotations, including those a PDF already has.

## What's new

- **Move and recolour annotations**: with no tool on, drag an annotation to another place on the page (it stays inside the page), and right-click for **Colour…**. This works for annotations already in the file and for those made in Plico; highlights, underlines and strike-outs stay on their text, and stamps and attachments keep their own look. Moving does not redraw the annotation, so a stamp or a bordered box looks as it did.
- **Resize annotations**: the corner handles of shapes, text boxes, drawings, lines and stamps change their size, for annotations already in the file and for drawings made in Plico. The border keeps its width, a text box wraps its text again and a stamp stretches with its picture; notes keep their size.
- **Text of text boxes (FreeText)** already in the file: **Edit Text…** from the right-click menu, and **Colour…** changes the colour of the text.

Everything undoes with `Ctrl+Z` and reaches the file only when you save.
